# Game loop

You inherit the Copper Acorn, an alchemy shop in Briarbridge. The guild asks you to prove the shop can supply the town.

| Cadence | Player actions |
| --- | --- |
| About 30 seconds | Gather a chosen quantity, buy ingredients, brew a potion, set a shelf price, serve a customer or check a merchant quote. |
| About 5 minutes | Complete a market day, replenish ingredients, observe prices and merchant cash, and deliver a quest order. |
| Each session | Advance the guild commission, help neighbours, improve the shop's stock, or visit the arena. Trading continues after the story objectives. |

Six ingredients produce three potions: Healing Tonic, Focus Draught and Restoration Elixir. Brewing consumes the recipe and a Copper fee. Selling transfers actual goods and Copper; listing alone is not a sale. Shop shelves display confirmed stock.

The main quest starts with brewing and selling a tonic, then supplying two Focus Draughts and one Restoration Elixir. Mira's tonic order and Rowan's water order are optional. Rewards come from finite funded balances.

Three merchants own separate wallets and inventory. More stock lowers their quotes; recent consumption raises demand. An empty Copper purse closes the shop; a fixed investment reopens it. Mana refills use a finite prefunded reserve, not a liquidity pool.

The arena has eight classes and three skills each, levels1–20, movement on a grid, and60-second turns. Combat ends at zero health. Only skills heal; there are no combat potions or item/Copper stakes. Training grants no XP and the chicken does not attack. Combat is server-authoritative; courtyard walking starts locally and reconciles with its confirmed position.

The game has no seven-day ending. Fast-forward mode was deliberately removed from the product.
